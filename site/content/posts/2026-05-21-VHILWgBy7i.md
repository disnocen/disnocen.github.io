I played with local AI, but now I wonder: 

> isn't *decentralized* AI better? 

Local AI is centralized, albeit on you

## waht is DecAI

*You clearly want open models so while the following can apply to frontier and
proprietary models, they are not my concern now* [^1]

[^1]: And btw, what's a *open* model anyway? how do open weights compare to
reading the source code directly if you don't access to the *training* source?

Open models run on _a lot_ of (inference) providers. why don't we just optimize
for it? Local AI is just *my inference provider* anyway, it's not *free* as in
free software/speech and certainly not free as in beer. a good model is as
costly as it gets and you will not likely a good quality from it. 

The stack:

- my computer
    - open harness
- decentralized inference 
    - open modelS


## The Cons (I can think of)

- The only reason why you wouldn't have external inference is privacy, but you
can anon your account for personal secrets (ofc they can still "correlate", but adulthood is tradeoffs understanding and acceptance )
    - what about company secrets? well, how possible is for the inference providers to capitalize on your requests, expeciallyif the work will be open sourced/published
- I don't think censorship is a real problem, as the availability of inference providers
improves (again going into a *renting* mindset instead of *owning* mindset
[^2])

[^2]: [tinyboxes](https://tinygrad.org/#tinybox) will be interesting to evaluate
further along the way...
